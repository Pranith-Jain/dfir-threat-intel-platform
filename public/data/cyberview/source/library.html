    About this library

    A mirror of ORKL's catalogue of public threat-intelligence reports and the actors they track. CyberView keeps each report's title, source, year, language and the links back to ORKL — never the report text, which you read on ORKL itself. Where CyberView AI (a Claude model) has read a report and the audit has checked it, a short summary is shown; stories about threat actors are reviewed before they appear and cite the reports every fact came from. Each summary and story has a Report an error link.

      Pick a report to see its source, the actors it names and the links to read it on ORKL.

      About the actor catalogue

      Threat-actor entries ORKL keeps from MITRE ATT&CK, ETDA, MISP Galaxy and Secureworks, with their aliases and tools. Open an actor to see its record, the reports in the library that name it and its ORKL profile.

        Pick an actor to see its aliases, tools, the reports in the library that name it and its profile on ORKL.

        Where this comes from

        The catalogue is mirrored from the Open Research Knowledge Library (ORKL), a community archive of public threat-intelligence reporting. The title, source and links are mirrored from ORKL, and each report is read on ORKL. Where CyberView AI (a Claude model) has read a report, its own short summary is shown, and for actors a story that a separate review passed; both are written by an AI model, and each has a Report an error link. Indicators of compromise are linked, never copied.
