How many events were recorded each day?

Last 28 days, unverified claims left out. A day's count can still grow as later sweeps find more.

Counts are events on file whose date falls in each week; one event can carry several sectors, products or tags. Names come from the sources and are not merged across spellings. Select a name to see its events in the archive.

Power grid: where were the most customer-hours without power in the last 30 days?

Context, not cyber events: outages are not attacks, and a cause is shown as the operator reports it. Figures are operator-reported and come from six operators only (Hydro-Québec, BC Hydro, UK Power Networks, Eskom, ERCOT, AEMO), so most of the world is missing. Customer-hours add up unplanned customers without power, sampled every 15 minutes; a customer is a meter, not a person, and operators count them differently, so compare a region with itself over time rather than with other regions. Load shedding and grid emergencies are levels, not customers, and are not in the bars. Computed by a script, not written.
