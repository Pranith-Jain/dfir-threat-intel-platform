The rules
  • Be useful to defenders: what you are seeing, what worked, a question, context on an event.
  • No claim that a named organisation or person was breached, at fault or behind an attack unless you link a public source that says so. Unsourced tips are passed to the research jobs as leads instead of being published.
  • No personal information, credentials, internal hostnames, indicators of compromise, exploit code or attack instructions.
  • No advertising, recruiting, abuse or harassment. At most 2 https links, no shortened links and no file downloads.
  • At most 5 posts a day, from GitHub accounts at least 3 days old. Found an error in an event? Use "Report an error" on the event instead.
  • Posts can be taken down later if they prove false or harmful. Every post has a Report link.
