## Tier 2 — Symlink double-counting (50 symlinks)

`api/src/lib/*.ts` are symlinks into `worker/lib/`. This is intentional (per
CLAUDE.md) but means any LOC tooling double-counts every shared loader. Exclude
symlinked paths in wc/sonar/coverage tooling rather than changing the layout.
